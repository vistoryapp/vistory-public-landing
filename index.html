Common Questions

Everything you
want to know.

What is Vistory?

Vistory is a GPS-based historical tourism app for iPhone and Android. When you physically visit a historical marker, GPS proximity unlocks a collectible historical icon with a verified narrative about that location. Think of it as Pokémon Go for history lovers and families.

How does GPS proximity work?

Vistory checks your GPS location every 15 seconds with accuracy under 50 meters required. When you're within range of a historical marker, your visit is verified automatically. You have to actually be there — no shortcuts, no spoofing.

What is a Silver Shared Discovery?

When your group visits a historical marker and a member can't be there — deployed overseas, away at college, recovering from illness, or simply couldn't make the trip — they automatically receive a Silver Shared Discovery icon. They can react in real time, and the Silver icon becomes a personal invitation to visit that marker themselves someday.

Who verifies the historical content?

Vistory partners with museums, historical societies, and state historic preservation offices to source and verify marker narratives. We never publish unverified historical claims.

Is Vistory free to use?

Yes. Vistory's free tier includes discovery of all historical markers, one group, one theme campaign, and all achievements. Explorer ($4.99/month) and Historian ($9.99/month) plans unlock unlimited groups, trips, and premium features. A 7-day free trial is available on paid plans.
